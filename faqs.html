Noir Space BH® Priority Planner

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Your priorities. Your pace. Your progress tracked privately in one place.

This is a private, focused monthly digital planning tool designed to help you organise what matters most.

1. What is Priority Planner designed to do?

Priority Planner helps you focus on meaningful priorities rather than manage an endless list of tasks.

Each month, you can organise up to 3 High Priorities and 6 Low Priorities, encouraging you to focus, take action and achieve your goals at your own pace.

2. Does Priority Planner work offline?

Yes. Priority Planner is designed to work offline, without requiring a continuous internet connection for its core planning features.

Your personal planning records are stored locally on your device, rather than automatically synchronised to cloud storage. Your information remains under your control.

3. Which devices support Priority Planner?

Priority Planner is currently available for compatible Android devices through Google Play.

An Apple iOS version release is planned for the future.

4. How do I protect my PIN and recovery code?

When creating your profile, choose a secure 4-digit PIN that you can remember.

IMPORTANT: When your recovery code is generated during initial setup, write it down by hand immediately and keep it somewhere safe.

  • Never share your PIN or recovery code.
  • Store your recovery code separately from your PIN.
  • Avoid keeping both in an unsecured location.

If you lose both your PIN and recovery code, you may be unable to regain access to your encrypted planner data. However, if you have your pin, you can re-generate a new recovery key.

5. Can I generate a new recovery code?

Yes. If you can still access your profile, open Settings → SETUP RECOVERY CODE.

Enter your existing profile PIN to generate a new recovery code. Write down the new code and store it securely. Your previous recovery code will no longer be valid. You can also use this key to change your pin.

6. How do monthly priority limits work?

Each calendar month has its own capacity of 3 High and 6 Low Priorities.

Completed and archived tasks continue to count towards that month's capacity. A new month provides fresh capacity. You can also amend a previous month's tasks, provided its capacity limits are respected.

Only deleting a task releases its occupied monthly slot.

7. What is the difference between Hide, Archive and Delete?

Hide: Removes a task from your main view without deleting its record. Useful for reducing visual clutter.

Archive: Organises completed tasks while preserving their history, monthly capacity and progress reporting.

Delete: Permanently removes a task from the planner and releases its occupied monthly slot.

Use Hide and Archive feature to keep your planner organised, and tidy without losing your record of progress.

8. How are Actions, Open, Completed and Overdue tasks tracked?

Actions this month: Track the total tasks you have created for each month.

Open: Tasks still in progress that have not passed their intended deadline.

Completed: Tasks you have finished, including those placed in the archive.

Overdue: Outstanding tasks identified by the planner's deadline and status rules.

The Calendar and Yearly Insights help you review your activity and achievements. Changing a deadline can move a task into another month or year, subject to available monthly capacity.

9. How do I protect or transfer my planner data?

Priority Planner provides an encrypted Export and Import feature. Regularly export a backup and save it somewhere secure, preferably outside the application on your device.

Data is stored locally, losing your device, uninstalling the application or clearing its storage could result in permanent data loss.

A backup is especially important before changing devices or resetting the app. Keep any credentials needed to access and restore your information.

10. What does CLEAR DATA do?

CLEAR DATA provides options for managing or removing locally stored information.

Clear Planner Data: Removes your planner entries while retaining your profile and access settings.

Reset Entire App: Removes your entire planner information and profile settings, returning the application to its initial setup state.

Important: These are destructive actions. Always export any information you wish to preserve before proceeding in this area.

11. Why can't I take screenshots inside Priority Planner?

The Android application includes screenshot and screen-recording restrictions to help protect sensitive personal information.

This is an intentional privacy feature, although it cannot prevent every possible method of capturing a screen.

12. Does Priority Planner support different languages and accessibility?

Yes. Priority Planner offers 17 language options for its interface.

Read Aloud is a basic English-language feature that reads the application’s main heading and text entered into the supported task input.

It does not read every screen or replace your device’s built-in screen reader.

Read Aloud may occasionally announce seconds that are not displayed in the task interface. Further improvements may be introduced in future updates.

13. What happens if I change or lose my Android device?

Because your planning records are stored locally, they do not automatically appear on another phone. Before changing devices, create a secure encrypted backup. Use the supported Import process to restore your information, and keep your PIN and recovery credentials safe.

Do not assume that uninstalling the app or resetting a device will preserve your records.

14. What does Priority Planner NOT do or replace?

Priority Planner is a personal organisation and productivity tool.

It does not replace:

  • Professional medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Emergency services or safety critical reminder systems.
  • Dedicated cloud backup or automatic device synchronisation.
  • Your own judgement, decisions or responsibility for completing tasks.

Priority Planner supports your planning journey. It does not make decisions or complete priorities on your behalf.

A Final Reminder

Protect your PIN. Write down your recovery code. Backup your data in a secure place.

Focus on your priorities, celebrate your progress along the way and make each month intentional.
