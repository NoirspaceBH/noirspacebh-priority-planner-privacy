Privacy Policy

Noir Space BH® Priority Planner

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. About This Notice

This notice explains how Noir Space BH Limited handles User information in the Noir Space BH® Priority Planner (“the Priority Planner”). This policy applies to the release. Noir Space BH Limited reviews it whenever the Priority Planner or its data handling changes.

The organisation updates this policy when the Priority Planner or applicable legal requirements change, and we will refresh the “Last updated” date above.

In this notice, “you” or “User” means a person who uses the Priority Planner. “We”, “us”, “our” or “the organisation” mean Noir Space BH Limited.

2. Data Controller and Contact

Noir Space BH Limited (company number 13240213, registered 167-169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF) is the controller for personal information you send us, such as support emails. Local priority planner data stays on your device and is not processed by us. Contact: issues@noirspacebh.com. We respond to rights requests within one month.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Priority Planner uses browser local storage to save information the User enters or creates.

  • Stored Elements: Profile names, tasks, priorities, deadlines, notes, language settings and related planner settings. The Priority Planner also stores protected information needed for PIN access and account recovery.
  • Tracking: The Priority Planner has no advertising, analytics or built-in crash reporting, and does not upload your planner records. Browser storage is used only to provide the features you request. It is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Security Note: Priority Planner records are stored locally on your device. PIN protection helps prevent unauthorised access but does not replace device security. Someone with access to your unlocked Planner or sufficient access to your device may still be able to access your information.

4. Optional Browser and Device Features

These features depend on browser or device settings. The Priority Planner does not save audio recordings as Priority Planner data.

  • Read Aloud: Text to speech uses browser or device features. Processing depends on the device, installed voices and provider.
  • Notifications: If supported and enabled, the Priority Planner may request permission to show reminder text. Notifications may be visible to other people who can see the User's screen or device.

5. Backups, Imports and Deletion

  • Backups: Users can export their Priority Planner data to a password protected backup JSON file. The exported file is stored on the User's device outside the Priority Planner's ongoing control. Users are responsible for protecting their backup files and passwords, keeping appropriate copies and deleting backups when no longer required. Users should not assume that any security measure provides absolute protection.
  • Imports: The Priority Planner supports backup imports and profile restoration. Importing a compatible backup may replace existing local planner data. Backup restoration may require the backup password and the original profile PIN. Security safeguards help prevent a backup from being imported into an unrelated profile. Users should check the backup and intended destination before confirming restoration.
  • Deletion: Users can use Clear Planner Data or Reset Entire App to remove the Planner data targeted by those controls. They do not promise to erase every item in browser storage or copies saved elsewhere.
  • Data Loss: Data can be lost if browser data is cleared, the app or browser profile is removed, the device is reset, or storage failure occurs. Deleting local data does not delete exported backups or emails already sent to the organisation.

6. Support and Issue Report Emails

  • Drafting: The Priority Planner prepares an email draft. It does not send the email automatically or save a copy of the report in the Planner.
  • Data Received: If the User sends the email, the organisation receives the message, the sender's email address and standard email metadata.
  • Security: Users must not include unnecessary personal information, a PIN, recovery code, passwords or sensitive Planner records in an email.
  • Legal Basis: The organisation processes support emails on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to queries and maintaining the product. We may also process or retain information where needed to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The User's email provider and the service hosting our support mailbox may process messages under their own terms.

7. Sharing and Retention

  • No Selling: The organisation does not sell personal information.
  • Access: The Priority Planner’s own code does not upload local records to the organisation. Access to support emails is limited to people who need them to handle the User's request or meet relevant business or legal responsibilities. Information may be disclosed where required by law.
  • Retention: The organisation keeps support emails for 12 months after the issue is resolved, and keeps business or legal records as needed. Local data remains on the device until the User deletes it through the Priority Planner or clears the relevant browser storage.

8. Your Privacy Rights

  • Local Data: Because local data stays on the device, the organisation cannot access, correct or delete it for the User. Users can manage it using the Priority Planner’s controls and browser or device settings.
  • Email Data: For personal information you email us, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, and may object to certain processing, subject to applicable law. We process issue emails on the basis of legitimate interests. Crash logs and screenshots may contain personal information, so please review them carefully before sending. We keep support emails for 12 months after the issue is resolved.
  • Complaints: Users in the UK can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. International users may contact their local data protection authority.

9. International Users and Third-Party Services

The Priority Planner is intended for distribution through the mobile app stores. These providers may collect information under their own privacy policies and terms. The organisation does not receive your locally stored records through these services.

Optional speech, notification and email features may use services provided by third parties. Their handling of information depends on the provider and the User's device settings. Users should review the relevant privacy notices before enabling these features.

Where optional contributions or purchases are available, payments are handled by the relevant payment provider or the mobile app stores. The Priority Planner does not collect or store payment card details.

10. Children

The Priority Planner is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. Parents or guardians who allow a child to use it are responsible for supervising that use. If we receive a child’s personal information by email, we will delete it.

11. User Responsibilities

The User is responsible for keeping their PIN and recovery information secure and for protecting access to their device and browser profile. Do not share the PIN or recovery code with other people.

The User should keep appropriate backups where the backup feature is available and check that a backup can be restored. Protect exported backup files and delete them when they are no longer needed.

The User is responsible for the information they choose to enter, including any personal information about other people. Avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for planning needs, and make sure there is an appropriate reason before recording information about someone else.

The User should understand that locally stored data may be lost if browser data is cleared, the app or browser profile is removed, the device is lost or damaged, storage fails, or access details cannot be recovered. Do not rely on the Priority Planner as the only place to store critical information.

The User is responsible for reviewing tasks and keeping information accurate and up to date
