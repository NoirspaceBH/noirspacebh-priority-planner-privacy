Child Safety Standards

At Noir Space BH, the safety and wellbeing of our users is a core priority. We are committed to maintaining a safe online environment and have a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

These Child Safety Standards outline our commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation, protecting children from harm, and complying with applicable laws and the Google Play Child Safety Standards.

1. Age Requirement

The former Noir Space BH community networking mobile application was intended for adults aged 18 years and over and is not currently available.

Age requirements for individual NSBH digital products will be stated where applicable. These product restrictions do not mean that all of our business, education or community engagement activities are necessarily limited to adults.

Before collecting personal information directly from children or young people through any school, mentoring or career pathways programme, NSBH will review appropriate safeguarding measures, lawful bases, privacy notices and any consent arrangements required for that activity.

2. Our Commitment

Noir Space BH does not permit any activity that exploits, endangers, or harms children.

We prohibit:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Grooming or attempting to establish inappropriate relationships with minors.
  • Solicitation of minors for sexual purposes.
  • Human trafficking involving children.
  • Sexual extortion, coercion, or blackmail involving minors.
  • Any content or behaviour that promotes, encourages, facilitates, or attempts to facilitate the abuse or exploitation of children.

Any account found to be engaging in these activities may be suspended or permanently removed without notice.

3. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Noir Space BH operates a zero-tolerance policy towards child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Where appropriate, we may:

  • Remove content that breaches these standards.
  • Suspend or permanently terminate user accounts.
  • Restrict access to our services.
  • Preserve relevant information where required by applicable law.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement agencies and competent authorities where legally required.

4. Reporting Concerns

Protecting children is a shared responsibility.

When available, users will be able to report:

  • User profiles.
  • Messages.
  • Images.
  • Videos.
  • Comments.
  • Any behaviour believed to violate these Child Safety Standards.

The Noir Space BH mobile application is currently unavailable while redevelopment is underway.

Before the mobile application relaunches, reporting functionality will be reviewed and implemented as required to support compliance with applicable laws and Google Play policies.

Until the mobile application becomes available, child safety concerns may be reported using the contact information provided below.

5. Content Moderation

Noir Space BH is committed to maintaining a respectful, safe, and responsible online environment.

Reported content may be reviewed to determine whether it breaches:

  • These Child Safety Standards.
  • Our Community Guidelines.
  • Our Terms and Conditions.
  • Applicable laws and regulations.

Content that breaches these standards may be removed, and user accounts may be suspended or permanently terminated where appropriate.

6. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Noir Space BH strictly prohibits the creation, possession, upload, sharing, distribution, promotion, solicitation, or attempted distribution of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Where we become aware of suspected CSAM or other unlawful child exploitation activity, we may take appropriate action, including reporting the matter to relevant law enforcement authorities where required by applicable law.

7. Compliance

Noir Space BH is committed to complying with applicable child protection legislation in the jurisdictions in which we operate.

We are also committed to complying with the Google Play Child Safety Standards where they apply to our services.

8. Helping Protect Children

We encourage everyone who interacts with NSBH services and activities to help protect children by reporting any suspected exploitation, grooming, or other unlawful activity involving minors.

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, you should contact your local law enforcement agency or emergency services immediately.

9. Mobile Application Status

The original Noir Space BH community networking mobile application is currently unavailable and its former Google Play listing has been removed. No relaunch date is currently confirmed.

NSBH is separately developing and distributing other digital products. Each product may have its own age requirements, privacy information and applicable safety measures. If the original community networking application is relaunched in the future, NSBH will review and implement the child safety measures required by applicable law and Google Play policies before it becomes available.

These Child Safety Standards are published as part of our ongoing commitment to transparency, responsible platform governance, and user safety.

10. Contact Our Child Safety Team

If you wish to report content, raise a child safety concern, or contact us regarding these Child Safety Standards, please contact:

Email: support@noirspacebh.com

If you believe a child is in immediate danger, please contact your local law enforcement agency or emergency services immediately.

11. Updates to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety Standards from time to time to reflect changes to applicable laws, regulations, our services, or Google Play requirements.

The latest version will always be published on the Noir Space BH website.
